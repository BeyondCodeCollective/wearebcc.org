What You Learn

Every week.

The tools are the vehicle. You practice these skills every week, and they stay with you when the tools change.

Think critically

An LLM is predictive. It makes an educated guess and does not think. Validate, don't trust. When the stakes are real, one agent does the work and a second checks it.

Set it up

Choose the voice you want back. Choose the platform that fits the task and say why. Know the boundary on safety, web access, approvals, and training data.

Listen deeply

Interview before you build. Hear the problem underneath the stated one, in the other person's words.

Communicate

With yourself and the AI, inside your org where your expertise is the value, and outside it through storytelling, research, and presentation.

Judge and defend

Know what good looks like before you test. Iterate on evidence. Know how to call it done, and explain it to anyone.

How It Runs

Built around your schedule.

Field Ready is modular, so it meets you where you are. Join the live kickoff at the time that works for your week, work through the content at your own pace, and learn alongside a small pod of people on the same path.

Weekly

Live kickoff

60 minutes, offered at multiple times. Your facilitator frames the week, teaches the core ideas, and works in the tool live with you.

3+ hrs a week

Self-guided content

Each unit says what you do, what you must be able to explain, what done looks like, and where people get stuck, with worked examples in different industries.

Pods of 5

Your pod

Five people learning side by side. Share what you're building, trade feedback, work through stuck points together, and cheer each other on. The lead role rotates, so everyone practices guiding the group.

Thursdays, optional

Office hours

Each pod's lead joins, and other members are welcome. Ten minutes on what's stuck this week, forty minutes of unsticking each other, ten minutes on the assignment.

Then

After the program

Graduates join the Beyond community layer: a network of other learners, potential mentors, and extra programming.
