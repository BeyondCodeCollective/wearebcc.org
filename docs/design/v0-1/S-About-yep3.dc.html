[ Why we’re here ]

We teach people how to think with AI, not simply how to use it.

The problem

Technology is reshaping nearly every industry, and powerful tools are reaching people faster than the knowledge, context and support needed to use them well. AI is one of those tools, not the whole story.

What we built

Free programs, live sessions and community for every generation, in person and online, with lifelong access as technology keeps changing. Learning is stronger together, and trust is built through place and relationships.
